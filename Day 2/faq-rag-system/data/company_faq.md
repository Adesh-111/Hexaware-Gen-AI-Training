# Company FAQs

## What are the standard working hours?
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM in your local office time zone, including a one-hour meal break. Teams may use flexible schedules with manager approval, provided employees attend required meetings and meet their responsibilities.

## How do I apply for leave?
Submit planned leave through the employee HR portal and notify your manager as early as possible. Planned leave should normally be requested at least five working days in advance. For unexpected illness or emergencies, contact your manager promptly and record the absence in the portal when you are able.

## How many paid leave days do employees receive?
Full-time employees receive paid leave according to the allowance shown in their employment agreement and the HR portal. Allowances can differ by location, tenure, and employment type. The HR portal is the authoritative source for your current balance.

## What is the work-from-home policy?
Employees may work remotely according to their team’s hybrid-work arrangement and with manager approval. They must remain available during agreed collaboration hours, protect company information, use approved equipment, and maintain a suitable working environment.

## How do I reset my company password?
Use the “Forgot password” option on the company sign-in page. Complete identity verification and create a new password that meets the displayed security requirements. If self-service recovery fails or your account is locked, contact the IT service desk.

## How do I contact IT support?
Open a ticket through the IT service portal and include the affected service, device name, time of the issue, screenshots when appropriate, and a clear description of what happened. For critical outages or suspected security incidents, use the urgent-support option in the portal.

## How do I report a security incident?
Report suspected phishing, lost devices, exposed credentials, malware, or unauthorized access to the Security team immediately using the security-incident option in the IT portal. Do not investigate the incident yourself, delete evidence, or share sensitive details in public channels.

## When is salary paid?
Salary is paid according to the payroll schedule for your employing country and entity. The payment date and payslip are available in the HR portal. If a payment is late or incorrect, open a confidential payroll ticket with HR.

## How can I update my bank or personal details?
Update your address, emergency contact, and permitted payroll details in the HR portal. Changes to legal name, tax information, or bank details may require supporting documents and identity verification. Never send bank information through ordinary email or chat.

## How are expenses reimbursed?
Submit business expenses through the expense portal with an itemized receipt, business purpose, project or cost center, and required approval. Claims should be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Personal purchases, missing receipts, and costs outside policy may not be reimbursed.

## How do I request new equipment or software?
Submit a request in the IT service portal with the business need and manager approval. IT will check security, licensing, compatibility, and available inventory. Do not install unapproved software or purchase company equipment personally unless Procurement has authorized it.

## Where can I find company policies?
Current company policies are published in the employee portal. Use the latest published version because downloaded copies may become outdated. Questions about interpretation should be directed to the policy owner or HR.

## How do I raise a workplace concern?
Employees can speak with their manager, HR representative, or the confidential ethics channel. Concerns may be raised without retaliation when made in good faith. For an immediate safety risk, contact local emergency services and company Security.

## What happens during onboarding?
New employees complete identity and payroll documentation, policy training, security setup, and role-specific orientation. The hiring manager coordinates the first-week schedule, required system access, team introductions, and initial goals.

## What should I do when leaving the company?
Coordinate the final working date with your manager and HR, complete the offboarding checklist, transfer work and records, return company equipment, and remove company data from approved personal devices. Access is removed according to the offboarding schedule.
